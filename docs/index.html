Common questions

How do I share with my family?

Open the Tribe tab, tap Set up home in the middle of the wheel, and create your home tribe. Share the invite code or link. Family members install Tribe Time, tap + and choose Join. Each person uses their own Apple ID.

Can I share a calendar with people outside my family?

Yes. Tap + on the Tribe wheel to start a tribe calendar for work, a team or friends. Only calendar events are shared there. To show one event from it on your family calendar, long-press the event and choose Share to….

Nothing is syncing

Make sure every phone is signed in to iCloud (Settings → [your name] → iCloud) and has internet access. Then open the tribe on the Tribe tab and tap Sync now.

How do I change the colors or app icon?

Tap the gear at the top-left of the Tribe tab.
