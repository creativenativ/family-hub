Privacy Policy

Tribe Time · Effective October 23, 2026

Tribe Time is a family organizer for calendars, chores, meals and household lists. It's built so your information stays with you and the people you choose to share with. We don't run servers that store your data, and we don't sell, rent or analyze it.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Tribe Time does not collect, receive or have access to any of your personal information. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Where your information is stored

  • On your device. Everything you enter is saved on your iPhone.
  • In your iCloud account. If you're signed in to iCloud, Apple's CloudKit keeps your data in sync across your own devices. This data lives in your private iCloud storage under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see it.
  • With people you invite. When you create or join a tribe, the items that tribe shares are stored in iCloud and visible to its members. A home tribe shares calendar events, chores, meals, groceries, budget, profiles and similar household items. A tribe calendar shares only calendar events. You decide who joins, and you can leave or delete a tribe at any time from the Tribe tab.

Invite codes

To let people join with a code, the app stores a small invite record in iCloud's public database. It contains the tribe's name, its invite code and a sharing link, and no other personal information. It's removed when the tribe's owner deletes the tribe.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to add profile pictures and scan recipes. Photos stay in the app and are shared only as part of your tribe's data.
  • Microphone: to record voice memos for family members.
  • Notifications: for event, chore and bill reminders, which are scheduled on your device.

Each permission is optional. You can change it anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Tribe Time is a general-audience app for families. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, leave or delete a tribe from the Tribe tab, or delete the app. To remove its iCloud data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
